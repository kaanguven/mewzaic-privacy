Mewzaic: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Mewzaic ("the game") is an Android game published by KFu ("we", "us"). You can reach us at ufukkaanguven@gmail.com.

In short

  • The game has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your progress is stored on your own phone.
  • When you buy something, Google Play takes the payment, and our service provider RevenueCat records the purchase so that the game can unlock what you bought.
  • We never see your payment details.

What the game stores on your phone

Your progress and settings: the levels you cleared and your stars, coins, boosters, lives, hats, Season Pass progress, the streak of your daily gift, your sound, music, vibration and language settings, a few counters such as wins and losses, and a record of the purchases the game has already processed. This data stays in the private storage of the game on your phone. We cannot read it. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the saved data of the game in your Google backup. Google runs that backup under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

You can buy coins, booster bundles, the Season Pass, the Starter Pack and the VIP subscription. The purchase is made in Google Play, which collects and processes your payment under Google's terms and privacy policy. We receive no card number or other payment detail.

To deliver and restore what you bought, the game uses RevenueCat, Inc. ("RevenueCat") as our service provider. RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf. The RevenueCat software in the game sends RevenueCat:

  • a random anonymous ID that the software creates on your phone (a new one is created if you reinstall the game);
  • details of your purchases from Google Play: which product, when, the price and currency, the Google Play order number and the purchase token;
  • technical information: the type of your device, the version of Android, and when you last opened the game;
  • your country, which RevenueCat works out from your purchase or from your IP address. RevenueCat states that it does not keep the IP address.

RevenueCat also receives updates about your purchases from Google Play, such as renewals, cancellations and refunds.

We use this data to unlock and restore what you bought, to manage subscriptions, to see how the game sells, and to answer you when you write to us about a purchase. RevenueCat stores customer data in data centers in the United States, so this data may leave your country. RevenueCat's privacy policy describes how it handles data it receives from games like this one.

What the game does not do

  • It shows no ads and uses no ad network or advertising ID.
  • It has no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • It does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or files.
  • It does not create an account.
  • We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers.

The game asks Android for four permissions: internet access and network state (to reach Google Play and RevenueCat), vibration (for the vibration you can switch off in Settings) and Google Play Billing (to sell the items above).

How long data is kept

On your phone: until you uninstall the game or clear its data. At RevenueCat: until we delete the record, which we do when you ask; otherwise as RevenueCat's own retention policy provides.

Your choices

  • Vibration, sound and music can be switched off in the Settings of the game.
  • A VIP subscription is cancelled in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Deleting your record does not cancel a subscription, so cancel first.
  • Deleting your purchase record: write to ufukkaanguven@gmail.com and send the Google Play order number of one of your purchases (it looks like GPA.1234-5678-9012-34567 and is in your Google Play receipt e-mail and your order history). We find the record by that number, delete it at RevenueCat and confirm by e-mail. If you never bought anything, RevenueCat only has the anonymous ID and the technical information above, which we cannot link to you, so there is nothing we could look up; uninstalling the game removes the ID from your phone.
  • Your rights. Depending on where you live you may have the right to ask for access to, correction of or deletion of personal data about you, to object to its processing and to complain to your data protection authority. Write to ufukkaanguven@gmail.com to use them.

Children

Mewzaic is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you think a child has bought something in the game, write to ufukkaanguven@gmail.com and we will delete the record.

Changes to this policy

When the game changes in a way that affects your data, for example if it adds ads or analytics, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
